I deleted something by mistake

Open the Photos app, go to Albums › Recently Deleted, select the items and tap Recover. Deleted items stay there for 30 days.

I deleted photos but my storage didn't change

iOS frees the space when items leave Recently Deleted: after 30 days, or right away if you delete them from Recently Deleted in Photos.

Sortroll only finds a few photos

You probably gave access to selected photos only. Go to Settings › Apps › Sortroll › Photos and choose Full Access.

Two photos aren't really duplicates

Tap the group and choose “Not duplicates — keep all”. Sortroll won't show that group again. To group fewer photos as similar, set Settings › Similar shots to Strict.

How does Sortroll pick the photo to keep?

Favourites are always kept. After that, Sortroll prefers the shot you picked in a burst, then open eyes and well-captured faces, then the sharpest and best-composed photo. For copies of the same picture it keeps the edited or highest-quality version. It all happens on your device with Apple's image analysis.

Purchases

Sortroll Unlimited is a one-time purchase, shared with your family through Family Sharing. If you reinstall or get a new device, open Settings in Sortroll and tap Restore Purchase. There's no subscription to cancel. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sortroll

Privacy policy

Short version: Sortroll collects no data. Your photos never leave your device.

Your photos

Sortroll needs access to your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos. It analyses your photos on your device with Apple's built-in Vision framework. Photos, thumbnails and analysis results are never uploaded, shared or sent anywhere. The analysis results are stored only in the app's own storage on your device so the next scan is faster, are excluded from backups, and are removed when you delete the app.

Photos are only deleted when you tap Delete and confirm in the iOS dialog. Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can restore them for 30 days.

No data collection

Sortroll has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. It does not need an internet connection. We (the developer) never receive any information about you or your photos.

Purchases

The one-time Sortroll Unlimited purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.
